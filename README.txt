Aubel Veterinary on the Go
==========================

## About

A mobile-friendly website for Aubel Veterinary on the Go, serving pet and farm
animal owners in Ogun State and Lagos. It brings service information, a basic
symptom urgency guide, a visit fee estimate, and contact options together on one
page.

## What the site does

- Lets visitors choose a species and age category before showing matching
	symptom prompts for dogs, cats, goats, sheep, cattle, rabbits, and poultry.
- For poultry, asks whether the flock is broiler or layer before displaying
	subtype-specific prompts.
- Provides a simple urgency assessment from the selected prompts and visitor's
	description, with an option to share a summary through WhatsApp or call the
	practice.
- Estimates a visit fee from the service and location selections.
- Shows a terms-and-conditions disclosure and requires acknowledgement.
- Provides a Digital Health Record form that previews owner, pet, and
	vaccination information, including a suggested follow-up window.
- Adapts its colors to the visitor's light or dark device theme.

The symptom guide is not a diagnosis and cannot rule out illness. Its prompts
are general observation cues, not an exhaustive, age-specific clinical review.
Visitors should contact a veterinarian for advice, especially when signs are
severe or worsening. Vaccination timing should be confirmed with a veterinarian.

## Digital record limitations

The record form currently creates a temporary summary in the browser only. The
site does not save or transmit owner or pet details. Email verification,
individual client accounts, persistent records, and a private administrator
dashboard are not available in this static version; those features require a
secure backend and authenticated access.
